Privacy notice

What this games portal keeps about you, why, for how long, and how to have it deleted.

Who runs it

This portal is a private, non-commercial project run by its owner. To ask about your data, use the Feedback page while signed in, choosing "General / Portal".

What is kept, and why

InformationWhyHow long
Username, email address, and your password in scrambled (hashed) form; it is never stored readable To give you an account and let you sign in Until you delete your account
When you registered and last signed in Shown on your Profile and to the portal's admins Until you delete your account
Your saved games So you can carry on playing from any device Until you delete the game or your account
Feedback you send To fix bugs and improve the games Kept after account deletion, with your name removed
Failed sign-in attempts (the username tried, and when) To stop people guessing passwords Deleted after one day
The network (IP) address an account was created from To stop automated mass sign-ups Deleted after one day
Admin actions on your account (for example a password reset), with the usernames involved A permanent record of who changed whose access Kept, as an audit record

The legal basis is providing the service you signed up for, and the portal's legitimate interest in keeping it secure.

In your browser

While you are signed in, your browser's local storage holds your sign-in token, your username, and when you were last active, so the games know who you are and can sign you out after a period without activity. There are no advertising or tracking cookies and no analytics.

Who else handles it

The site is hosted by Vercel and the database by Supabase, who process it only to run the service. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Your rights

  • Delete your account and every saved game yourself, at any time, from your Profile page.
  • Change your email address or password on the same page.
  • Ask for a copy of what is held about you, or for it to be corrected, through the Feedback page.
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you are unhappy with how your data is handled.
